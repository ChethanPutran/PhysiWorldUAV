Light Breeze: 6–11 km/h (4–7 mph) – Leaves rustle, wind felt on face. 
Gentle Breeze: 12–19 km/h (8–12 mph) – Leaves and small twigs move constantly. 
Moderate Breeze: 20–29 km/h (13–18 mph) – Raises dust, small branches move. 
Fresh Breeze: 30–39 km/h (19–24 mph) – Small trees sway, crested wavelets on water. 
Strong Breeze: 40–49 km/h (25–31 mph) – Large branches move, umbrellas difficult to use. 
Near Gale: 50–61 km/h (32–38 mph) – Whole trees in motion, walking against wind is inconvenient. 
Gale: 62–74 km/h (39–46 mph) – Twigs break off trees, walking generally impeded. 
Strong Gale: 75–88 km/h (47–54 mph) – Slight structural damage, roofing tiles may blow off. 
Storm: 89–102 km/h (55–63 mph) – Trees uprooted, considerable structural damage. 
Violent Storm: 103–117 km/h (64–72 mph) – Widespread damage to structures. 
Hurricane: 118+ km/h (73+ mph) – Severe structural damage, widespread devastation
