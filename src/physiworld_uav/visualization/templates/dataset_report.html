What each episode is for

Split role decides how an episode is used. Colour shows the kind of distribution shift it carries: in-distribution (ID), one of five OOD wind or physics shifts, or the physics-validation set.

Scenarios

Scenario template per episode. Open-loop programs excite the rotors or body rates directly; closed-loop ones track a reference.

Parts of the plan

Each part is a block of groups with its own domain sampling, wind regime and counterfactual template.

Wind

Mean horizontal wind speed per episode, by wind category. The dashed line is the ID limit of 4 m/s; OOD magnitude episodes sit above it. Dots are jittered vertically only to avoid overlap.

Vehicle domains

Sampled physical factors, one dot per distinct domain, relative to nominal (1.0). The shaded bands mark ID (±10 %) and the OOD shell (10–20 %). Mass is shown as a factor of the nominal 0.716 kg mass, not the base-link mass.

Counterfactual pairs

Groups share one seed and differ in one factor. Wind-scale siblings replay the same plan at 0, 0.5 and 1.0 times the wind. Domain siblings (physics validation) keep the wind and change the vehicle.
